Privacy policy

Your data stays on your phone.

Ferro collects no personal data. There is no account, no server and no tracking.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What Ferro stores

Your profile, goals, equipment, workouts, pain check-ins and progress are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We have no copy and no way to read them.

What Ferro sends

Nothing. Ferro has no backend. The app does not use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

Camera and on-device AI

If you scan your equipment, the camera image is processed on your device to recognise it and is not uploaded or kept. Optional AI features use the model built into your phone's operating system and run on the device.

Backups

Your phone's own system backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include Ferro's data, under that service's terms and your settings.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

Ferro is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: Support.
