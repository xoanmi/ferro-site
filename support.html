How can we help?

Questions, bugs or ideas for Ferro. We read every message.

Contact

Open a request at github.com/xoanmi/ferro-site/issues. Include your device model, iOS or Android version and the Ferro version from Settings.

Ferro has no account and no servers, so we cannot see your data. Please do not share health details you would rather keep private.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Ferro works without sign-up. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Does Ferro work offline?

Yes. Planning, logging and progress all run on the device, with or without a connection.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Your data lives only on your device. Your phone's own device backup includes it. Ferro never sends it anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. That removes everything Ferro stored. There is nothing kept anywhere else.

Equipment scanning does not work.

Scanning uses on-device image recognition that some phones do not support. You can always add your equipment manually from the equipment screen.

Is Ferro medical advice?

No. Ferro is a training tool, not a medical device. If something hurts, stop and talk to a health professional.
